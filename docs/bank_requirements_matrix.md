**Research date:** August 25, 2026  
**Scope:** Canada’s Big Six banks; data, AI, analytics, technology, and risk roles most relevant to this portfolio.

The evidence is deliberately separated into **live role-specific postings** and **official program-level signals**. A general student page is not presented as an open data role.

## Strongest cross-bank themes

1. **Core tools:** Python, SQL, Excel, Tableau/Power BI or equivalent BI platforms.
2. **End-to-end data work:** extraction, transformation, validation, aggregation, modelling, and reporting.
3. **Risk and controls:** credit/operational risk awareness, data accuracy, governance, and responsible judgment.
4. **Business translation:** concise insights, executive storytelling, recommendations, and non-technical communication.
5. **Delivery habits:** curiosity, ownership, teamwork, time management, adaptability, and attention to detail.
6. **Emerging technology:** AI/ML, generative AI, automation, cloud, and enterprise-scale data environments.

## Eligibility reminders

- Term, school-return, graduation, work-authorization, location, transcript, and application-package rules vary by posting.
- RBC’s cited four-month roles require a January–April 2027 placement and either a return to school afterward or a mandatory work term needed to graduate.
- CIBC’s cited portal is specifically for self-identified First Nations, Inuit, or Métis students; it is included as a current requirements signal, not as a recommendation for anyone outside that eligibility group.
- Always re-open the official posting immediately before applying because deadlines and status can change.
